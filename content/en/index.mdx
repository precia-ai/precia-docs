# PRECIA User Guide

PRECIA helps medical staff and hospital teams run clinical cases with the support of artificial intelligence (AI) analysis, without replacing a doctor's judgment. Every AI result must still be reviewed and validated by a doctor before it is used as a reference.

This guide is written for everyday users: doctors, unit admins, organization admins, and platform admins. There is no programming jargon here, everything is explained the same way you would explain it to a colleague.

## Where to start?

If this is your first time using PRECIA, start with the **Getting Started** section. It covers how to log in, how the app is laid out, and what your user role means.

## Guide overview

- **Getting Started**: logging in, learning the layout, understanding user roles.
- **Dashboard**: a quick overview of your unit/organization the moment you open the app.
- **Clinical Cases**: creating a new case, uploading files, importing many cases at once, viewing AI results, and reviewing/validating as a doctor.
- **AI Modules**: which analysis modules are available and how to enable them for a specific unit.
- **Unit Management**: managing units/departments and their members.
- **User Management**: adding new users and assigning their roles.
- **Reports & Analytics**: understanding the usage statistics dashboard.
- **History & Audit**: viewing a log of past activity.
- **AI Adoption Evaluation**: seeing how much AI results are used and trusted by doctors in daily practice.
- **SIMRS Integration**: connecting PRECIA to an existing hospital information system.
- **Account Settings**: updating your profile details, password, and personal account preferences.
- **Organization & Platform**: for organization admins and platform admins only, managing settings that apply organization-wide or platform-wide.
- **Help**: frequently asked questions and how to contact support.

If a step is confusing or does not match what you see in the app, check the **Help** section for how to reach the support team.
