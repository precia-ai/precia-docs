# Organizations & Platform Overview

The **Platform** area is a dedicated workspace for platform administrators (Super Admin and Platform Admin roles). Unlike the regular organization area, which only shows data for a single organization, this area sees **every organization** registered in PRECIA at once.

This page explains how to enter the Platform area, what it contains, and how to tell a cross-organization view apart from a regular single-organization view.

## Who can access it

Only accounts with the following roles see the Platform menu in the sidebar:

- **Super Admin (SUP)** — full access, used for development and demos.
- **Platform Admin (PAD)** — the official administrator at platform scope, holding cross-organization account and tenant management.

Organization admins (SAD) and roles below them never see this menu. If your account lacks access, navigating directly to `/platform/...` redirects you back to the regular dashboard.

## Entering Platform Mode

1. Log in as usual with an account holding the Super Admin or Platform Admin role.
2. Open any Platform area link, for example the Organizations menu in the sidebar (it appears automatically if your account has access).
3. Once inside, the sidebar switches to the **Platform Mode sidebar**. This is your signal that you are working across organizations, not inside a single one.

![Platform Mode sidebar with the "Platform Mode" badge and a link back to the organization](/screenshots/en/organisasi-platform/index/01-sidebar-mode-platform.png)

Note the top section of the sidebar highlighted with a blue box above:

- The **Platform Mode** label confirms you are in the cross-organization area.
- The **Back to Organization** link takes you back to the regular single-organization dashboard at any time.

## Menu inside the Platform area

Below the Platform Mode label, the sidebar lists:

- **Organizations** — a list of every organization (tenant) registered in PRECIA: code, name, type, active/inactive status, and edit/deactivate actions. See the "Managing Organizations" page for the step-by-step.
- **SIMRS Onboarding** — a wizard for connecting a new organization's hospital information system (SIMRS) to PRECIA.
- **Analytics** — usage statistics across every organization.
- **Users & Access** — cross-organization user account management. This menu deliberately lives under Platform rather than under a single organization, since managing accounts can span more than one organization at once.
- **Audit** — activity and compliance logs. This workspace is exactly the same component as the Audit page inside a regular organization, but its data scope is determined server-side by your account's permissions, it may cover one organization or all of them.

## The "Viewing every organization" notice

Some pages in the Platform area reuse the exact same component as their organization-scoped counterpart, the Audit page being one example. Because the layout is identical, you cannot tell from the screen alone whether the data shown covers a single organization or all of them. To solve this, PRECIA adds a **scope notice banner** at the top of those pages.

![The "Viewing every organization" banner above the Platform Audit page](/screenshots/en/organisasi-platform/index/02-banner-lintas-organisasi.png)

This blue banner reading **"Viewing every organization"** appears on pages such as Audit and Users & Access when opened from the Platform area. It reads:

> This page is not limited to a single organization. The same workspace shows only one organization when opened from the organization menu.

If you don't see this banner on a given page, the data shown is already automatically limited to a single organization (for example because your account's permissions are restricted to one organization, or because the page is always cross-organization by nature and needs no extra notice, like the Organizations list itself).

## Important notes

- Any action taken in the Platform area (deactivating an organization, editing a cross-organization user account, etc.) affects the organization it belongs to, not just the organization you logged in from. Make sure you really intend to do it before confirming.
- The **Back to Organization** link in the sidebar is always available to leave Platform Mode at any time, no need to log out.
- For detailed steps on each menu, see the separate pages: "Managing Organizations", "Cross-Organization Users", "Platform Analytics", "Platform Audit", and "New SIMRS Onboarding".
