# Help

This section is where you look for answers when something about PRECIA is unclear, or when you need to reach the support team directly.

## What's in this section

- **[Frequently Asked Questions](/bantuan/pertanyaan-umum)**: a collection of the questions users ask most often, from how to log in, why an AI result is not showing up, to why a certain button cannot be clicked. Check this section first before contacting support, the answer is likely already there.
- **[Contacting Support](/bantuan/menghubungi-dukungan)**: how to reach the support team when your issue is not covered in the FAQ, including what information to prepare so your issue can be handled faster.

## When to come here

- A button or menu item cannot be clicked and it is not clear why.
- An AI analysis result never shows up even though the case has been uploaded.
- You forgot your password or cannot log in.
- You see an error message you do not understand.
- You need access to a feature or unit you cannot currently reach.

If reading the FAQ does not resolve your issue, move on to the Contacting Support page to find out how to reach the team that can help.
