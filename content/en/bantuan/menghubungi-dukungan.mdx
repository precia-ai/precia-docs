# Contacting Support

If your issue is not answered on the [Frequently Asked Questions](/bantuan/pertanyaan-umum) page, the next step is to reach out to whoever can help. This page explains who to contact first, what information to prepare, and how to reach official PRECIA support.

## Step 1: Check with your organization's admin first

Before contacting PRECIA support, many day-to-day issues can actually be resolved faster by an admin within your own organization, since they manage access and settings at the organization or unit level.

- **Can't access a specific unit, or need a new role** (e.g. becoming an AI result validator): contact your **unit admin** or **organization admin**. They manage who belongs to each unit and what role each member holds.
- **A specific AI module doesn't appear or can't be used in a unit**: contact your **organization admin**, since enabling or disabling an AI module for a unit is configured at the organization level.
- **Forgot password and there is no self-service reset option**: contact your organization admin to have your account access recreated.

If, after checking with your organization admin, the issue turns out not to be an access setting (e.g. a system error, a page that fails to load, or AI results that never appear), proceed to PRECIA support below.

## Step 2: Prepare information before contacting support

To get your issue resolved faster, prepare the following information before submitting a report:

1. **Organization name (tenant)** you work in.
2. **Account email** used to log in.
3. **Page or feature** that has the issue (e.g. "transaction detail page", "uploading a new case in the Cardiology unit").
4. **What happened**, described as clearly as possible (e.g. "after clicking the Send to AI button, the page just sits there with no message at all").
5. **When it happened**, including the approximate date and time, so the support team can trace the logs from that period.
6. **Error message**, if one appeared (screenshot or copy the exact text).
7. **Transaction number or case ID** affected, if the issue relates to a specific transaction or AI result.

Screenshots help a lot. Capture the problematic page before doing anything else, so the original state isn't lost.

## How long until a response

Response time depends on how urgent the issue is and the service agreement in place at your organization. For issues that directly disrupt clinical work (e.g. being completely unable to access a patient's case), state the urgency clearly when reporting so it can be prioritized.

## Things to keep in mind

- Never share your account password with anyone, including the support team. Support will never ask for your password.
- If the issue involves sensitive patient data, share only what's necessary (e.g. a case number or transaction ID); avoid copying entire patient records into a report through an unsecured channel.
- After reporting an issue, note down the ticket or reference number (if the support channel provides one) to make follow-up easier.
