# Creating a New Case

A clinical case is a single patient examination that will be processed by AI. This page walks through creating a new case from scratch, until it appears in the transaction worklist and is ready for file upload.

## 1. Open the Clinical page

Click **Clinical** in the left menu. If your account has access to more than one unit, you'll be asked to pick a unit first (see the **Getting to Know the Interface** page for how unit selection works). Once a unit is active, you'll see the transaction worklist below.

![Transaction worklist with the New Transaction button](/screenshots/en/kasus-klinis/membuat-kasus-baru/01-worklist.png)

Click **+ New Transaction** in the top right to open the case creation form.

## 2. Fill in the Create Clinical Transaction form

The form opens as a panel on the right side of the screen, split into four sections: **Assignment**, **Patient Information**, **Examination**, and **Notes**.

![Empty Create Clinical Transaction form](/screenshots/en/kasus-klinis/membuat-kasus-baru/02-drawer-kosong.png)

### Assignment

- **Unit** (required) — the clinical unit responsible for this examination. Only units you have access to appear in the list. The case will follow whichever AI modules are currently active for that unit.

### Patient Information

- **Patient MRN** (required) — the patient's medical record number, e.g. `MRN-000123`.
- **Patient Name** (required) — the patient's full name.
- **Date of Birth** (optional) — leave blank if not yet known.
- **Gender** (optional) — choose one: Male, Female, Other, or Unknown.

Patient data entered here is confidential. Only authorized users within the same unit can view it.

### Examination

- **Priority** — choose one: Low, Normal, High, or Urgent. Defaults to Normal. Priority helps the team decide which cases in the worklist need attention first.

### Notes

- **Examination Notes** (optional) — additional clinical context, referral reason, or relevant history for this examination.

Example of a fully filled-in form:

![Fully filled-in Create Clinical Transaction form](/screenshots/en/kasus-klinis/membuat-kasus-baru/03-drawer-terisi.png)

**Important**: this form has no manual AI module picker. The case automatically receives an examination slot for every AI module currently active on the selected unit (for example, a cardiology unit with three active ECG modules will immediately create three analysis slots). If a unit has no active AI modules yet, ask your unit admin or organization admin to enable them first (see the documentation on per-unit AI module settings).

## 3. Click Create Transaction

Once every required field is filled in, click **Create Transaction** at the bottom of the panel. If any field is invalid (e.g. an empty MRN or a badly formatted date of birth), the system shows an error message under the relevant field and the form won't be saved until it's fixed.

On success, you're automatically taken to the detail page of the newly created case. Its status is recorded as **Unprocessed**, with the priority and the list of AI modules automatically assigned based on the unit.

![Case detail page after successful creation](/screenshots/en/kasus-klinis/membuat-kasus-baru/04-hasil-sukses.png)

## What's next

This new case can't be analyzed by AI yet until patient files are uploaded for each module. See the **Uploading Patient Files** page to continue.

## Cancelling a case

If a case was created by mistake, open its detail page and look for the cancel option there. A case that has already been sent to a doctor for review can no longer be cancelled by yourself, contact your unit admin instead. See the **Cancelling a Case** page for full details.
