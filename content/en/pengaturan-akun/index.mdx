# Settings Menu

The **Settings** page is a hub for accessing all your account preferences, profile, and security settings. From this single page, you can jump to other settings pages without hunting through the sidebar.

## 1. Opening the Settings page

Click **Settings** in the left sidebar ("General" group). The page shows a "Settings" title with a short description, followed by a grid of shortcut cards.

![Settings page showing a grid of shortcut cards to Profile Information, Password & Security, Units, AI Registry, and SIMRS Integration](/screenshots/en/pengaturan-akun/index/01-hub-pengaturan.png)

## 2. Available cards

Each card has a title, a short description, and a blue action link with an arrow icon. Click the card title or the action link to open the related page.

- **Profile Information** - Open Profile. Takes you to the Profile page's account information section, where you can view and update your email, full name, username, and other account details.
- **Password & Security** - Change Password. Takes you to the Profile page's security section, where you change your account password.
- **Units** - Manage Units. Takes you to the Units page, where you manage clinical units, view members, and configure unit settings.
- **AI Registry** - Open AI Registry. Takes you to the AI Registry page, where you view registered AI modules, lifecycle status, and module configurations.
- **SIMRS Integration** - Manage SIMRS connection. Takes you to the SIMRS integration settings page, where you configure connectors such as Khanza, MEDICCA, or Open Hospital for your organization.

## 3. Note on which cards appear

Not every user sees the same cards. The **Units** card only appears if your account has permission to view units (either your own unit or all units in the organization). The **AI Registry** card only appears if your account has permission to view all AI modules. The **Profile Information**, **Password & Security**, and **SIMRS Integration** cards always appear for every user role.

If you do not see the **Units** or **AI Registry** card, your account likely lacks permission for that feature. Contact your organization admin if you believe you should have access.
