# Profile and Security

The **Profile** page shows your account information in PRECIA and is where you update your personal details and change your password. Every user, whether a doctor, nurse, unit admin, organization admin, or platform admin, has their own Profile page that only shows their own account data.

To open this page, click **Profile** in the left-side navigation, under the **General** menu group.

## Viewing and editing account information

The **Profile** card at the top of the page shows all your account information.

![Profile page with the Email, Full Name, and Username fields marked with blue boxes](/screenshots/en/pengaturan-akun/profil-dan-keamanan/01-informasi-akun.png)

There are two kinds of fields on this card:

**Editable fields:**

1. **Email** — the email address linked to your account.
2. **Full Name** — your name as displayed throughout the application.
3. **Username** — the username used to log in.

**Read-only fields (cannot be changed from this page):**

- **User ID** — your account's unique system code.
- **Organization** — the name of the organization your account belongs to.
- **Status** — account status (Active, Locked, Pending Activation, or Inactive).
- **Role(s)** — the role(s) your account holds in this organization.
- **Last Login** — the last time you successfully logged in.
- **Created At** — the date this account was first created.

### Steps to update your profile

1. Edit the **Email**, **Full Name**, and/or **Username** fields as needed.
2. The **Save Profile** button is only clickable once at least one of these fields has changed. As long as nothing has changed, the button stays disabled (grayed out).
3. Click **Save Profile** to save your changes.
4. On success, a confirmation message **"Profile updated successfully."** appears above the button.

If you want to discard unsaved changes and restore the fields to the last saved data, click the **Reset** button.

**Note:** Email, Full Name, and Username are required. If any of these fields is left empty and you try to save, PRECIA shows an error message and the changes are not saved.

## Changing your password

The **Change Password** card sits below the Profile card. Scroll down to see it.

![Change Password card with the Current Password, New Password, Confirm New Password fields and the Update Password button marked with blue boxes](/screenshots/en/pengaturan-akun/profil-dan-keamanan/02-ubah-kata-sandi.png)

This card has three fields:

1. **Current Password** — the password you currently use to log in.
2. **New Password** — the replacement password you want. At least 8 characters.
3. **Confirm New Password** — retype the exact same new password, to make sure there is no typo.

### Steps to change your password

1. Fill in all three fields above.
2. Click the **Update Password** button.
3. On success, a message **"Password changed successfully."** appears, and all three fields are automatically cleared.

If the process fails, PRECIA shows an error message above the button, depending on the cause:

- **Fields not complete** — a message asks you to fill in all password fields first.
- **Confirm New Password does not match New Password** — a message says the confirmation does not match.
- **Current Password entered is wrong** — a message reads **"Incorrect Current Password."**
- **New password does not meet security requirements** (for example, fewer than 8 characters) — a rejection message from the system appears.

**Important notes:**

- The password change takes effect immediately once saved successfully. There is no way to undo it after the password has changed.
- After changing your password, use the new password for your next login. Your current active session on this device is usually not interrupted right away, but keep the new password stored somewhere safe.
- If you forget your Current Password and cannot fill in the first field, this page cannot help you. Contact your organization admin or unit admin to have a new password set for you, as described on the [Log In to Your Account](/memulai/masuk-ke-akun) page.
