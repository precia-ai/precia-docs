# Signing In

The login page is the entry point to PRECIA. Every user, whether a doctor, nurse, unit admin, organization admin, or platform admin, signs in through this same page using their username or email and password.

## How to log in

1. Open the PRECIA address given to you by your organization admin (for example `https://app.precia.site` or your organization's own address). You will see the login page below.

   ![Empty PRECIA login page, with the Username or Email field, Password field, and Sign in button highlighted](/screenshots/en/memulai/masuk-ke-akun/01-halaman-login.png)

2. Fill in the **Username or Email** field with the username or email address registered for you.
3. Fill in the **Password** field with your password.

   ![Filled login form with username/email and password, Sign in button highlighted](/screenshots/en/memulai/masuk-ke-akun/02-form-terisi.png)

4. Click the **Sign in** button.

If the username/email and password you entered are correct, you are taken straight to the main page for your role in that organization.

## If login fails

If the username/email or password you entered is wrong, PRECIA shows the message **"Invalid email or password. Please try again."** right above the Sign in button. The form stays on the same page, and the username/email and password fields keep their values so you can check and try again.

![Error message after a failed login attempt, highlighted with a blue box above the Sign in button](/screenshots/en/memulai/masuk-ke-akun/03-login-gagal.png)

If this message keeps appearing even though you are sure the details are correct, the likely causes are:

- A typo in the username, email, or password (password is case-sensitive).
- Your account has not been created yet, or has not been activated by your organization/unit admin.
- Your account has been temporarily deactivated by an admin.

Contact your organization or unit admin to check your account status.

## First-time login

PRECIA does not offer self-service account registration. For new users, your organization or unit admin creates the account first, then shares your initial username/email and password with you for your first login.

If you do not have an account yet, the login card shows **"Don't have an account? Contact your administrator."** near the bottom of the form.

## Forgot password

PRECIA does not currently have a self-service password reset feature on the login page. If you forget your password, contact your organization or unit admin to have a new password created for you.

## Signing out

The **Logout** button is in the left-hand navigation menu, at the bottom of the **General** menu list, once you are logged in. Click it to end your session and return to the login page.

Always sign out when using a shared computer, for example a computer in a nurses' station or admin room, so patient data and your access cannot be used by anyone else.
