# Getting to Know the Layout

After you log in, you will see a sidebar menu on the left side of the screen and the main content area on the right. This page explains the parts of that sidebar.

## Main menu

The **Menu** section lists the pages you can access: Dashboard, Clinical, Validation, Units, AI Registry, Analytics, Users & Access, Audit, and Evaluation.

![Main menu in the dashboard sidebar](/screenshots/en/memulai/mengenal-tampilan/01-dashboard-sidebar.png)

The menu items shown depend on your role. Each item only appears if your account has permission for that page, so not every user sees the same list. For example, a doctor who only handles clinical cases usually will not see the **Users & Access** menu, since managing user accounts is not part of that job.

## Platform section (platform admins only)

If your account is a Platform Admin or Super Admin, an extra **Platform** section appears below the main menu, with a **Switch to Platform** button. This button takes you out of the regular organization view into the cross-organization view, where you manage every organization (tenant) registered in PRECIA.

After pressing **Switch to Platform**, the sidebar changes completely. The top shows a **Platform Mode** badge with a **Back to Organization** link, followed by the platform-specific menu below it: Organizations, SIMRS Onboarding, Analytics, Users & Access, and Audit. Every page here spans all organizations, not just one.

![Sidebar in Platform Mode on the Organizations page](/screenshots/en/memulai/mengenal-tampilan/02-mode-platform.png)

> **Note:** the **Platform Mode** badge is an important marker so you do not confuse managing a single organization's data with cross-organization data. Press **Back to Organization** any time to return to the regular view.

## General section

Below the main menu (or the platform menu), there is a **General** section containing:

- **Profile** — view and edit your own account details.
- **Settings** — account preferences.
- **Help** — help using the application.
- **Logout** — end your login session.

## Account identity card

At the bottom of the sidebar, there is a small card showing your name, email, and the organization currently active (for example, "System Health Organization"). This card helps confirm you are working in the correct organization, especially if your account has access to more than one.

## Language and appearance

At the very bottom of the sidebar there are two quick switches:

- **Language** — switch between Indonesian (ID) and English (EN). All application text changes to match your choice.
- **Appearance** — switch between light and dark mode.

Both settings take effect immediately, no page reload needed.
