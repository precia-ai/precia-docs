# Getting Started

This section is for anyone opening PRECIA for the first time. Work through the following three pages in order before moving on to your daily tasks.

## Step 1: Log In to Your Account

Open **[Log In to Your Account](/memulai/masuk-ke-akun)** to learn:

- How to log in for the first time using the email and password provided by your organization admin.
- How to reset your password if you forget it.
- What to do if login keeps failing (for example, wrong organization, an account that is not yet active, or a locked account).

## Step 2: Get to Know the Interface

Open **[Get to Know the Interface](/memulai/mengenal-tampilan)** to learn:

- The main parts of the screen after you log in: the sidebar, the top header, and the main content area.
- How to switch your active unit if you belong to more than one unit.
- How to read notifications and use the account profile menu.

## Step 3: Understand User Roles

Open **[Understand User Roles](/memulai/mengenal-peran)** to learn:

- The list of roles in PRECIA (for example doctor, unit admin, organization admin, platform admin) and what each role can do.
- How to check your own role inside the application.
- Why some sidebar menus may differ from your coworkers', since each role has different menu access.

## After These Three Steps

Continue to the section that matches your daily work:

- Doctors or nurses handling patient cases: go to **[Clinical Cases](/kasus-klinis)**.
- Unit admins managing team composition and AI modules for a unit: go to **[Unit Management](/manajemen-unit)**.
- Organization admins managing users and access rights: go to **[User Management](/manajemen-pengguna)**.
- Want a one-screen summary of your daily work: go to **[Dashboard](/dashboard)**.

If you run into an issue not covered anywhere else, check the **[Help](/bantuan)** section.
